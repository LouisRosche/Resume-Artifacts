# Conflict Resolution Circle Protocol

## Purpose
Address conflicts and disagreements in a structured, supportive environment that promotes understanding and resolution.

## Duration
60-90 minutes

## Pre-Circle Preparation
- Meet individually with each party involved
- Assess readiness for circle process
- Explain circle process and expectations
- Identify support people if needed
- Prepare key questions

## Circle Structure

### 1. Opening & Guidelines (10 minutes)
- Welcome all participants
- Review circle guidelines
- Establish ground rules:
  - Use "I" statements
  - Focus on impact, not intent
  - No interrupting
  - Respect the process
  - Commit to working toward resolution

### 2. Understanding the Situation (20-30 minutes)
**Round 1: What Happened**
- Each person shares their perspective
- No interruptions or responses yet
- Facilitator summarizes key points

**Round 2: Impact**
- "How has this situation affected you?"
- "What have you been feeling?"

### 3. Identifying Needs (15-20 minutes)
**Prompts:**
- "What do you need to move forward?"
- "What would help you feel heard/safe/respected?"
- "What would repair look like for you?"

### 4. Problem-Solving (15-20 minutes)
- Brainstorm solutions together
- Discuss realistic actions
- Create specific agreements
- Identify support needed

### 5. Agreement & Closing (10-15 minutes)
- Summarize agreed-upon actions
- Set follow-up date
- Check-in: "How are you feeling about this agreement?"
- Close with appreciation for participation

## Key Questions for Facilitator

### For Understanding
- "Can you help me understand what happened from your perspective?"
- "What was going through your mind at that moment?"
- "How did that make you feel?"

### For Moving Forward
- "What would you like the other person to understand?"
- "What are you willing to do differently?"
- "What support do you need to follow through?"

## Facilitator Guidelines

### Do:
- Remain neutral and impartial
- Validate feelings without taking sides
- Keep the process moving forward
- Summarize and clarify
- Focus on future and solutions

### Don't:
- Judge or blame
- Rush the process
- Force resolution
- Make decisions for participants
- Share personal opinions

## Safety Considerations
- Ensure power dynamics are addressed
- Have a plan for if someone becomes dysregulated
- Know when to pause or stop the circle
- Follow up with individual support if needed

## Follow-Up Protocol
- **24 hours**: Check in with each participant
- **1 week**: Review progress on agreements
- **2 weeks**: Follow-up circle if needed
- **1 month**: Final check-in

## Documentation
- Record attendance
- Document agreements made
- Track follow-up dates
- Note outcomes for program evaluation
