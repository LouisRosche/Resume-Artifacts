# Community Building Circle Protocol

## Purpose
Build trust, strengthen relationships, and create a sense of belonging within the community.

## Duration
45-60 minutes

## Preparation
- Arrange chairs in a circle (no tables)
- Prepare talking piece
- Have check-in questions ready
- Ensure a quiet, comfortable space

## Circle Structure

### 1. Opening (5 minutes)
- Welcome participants
- Explain circle guidelines:
  - Respect the talking piece
  - Listen without interrupting
  - Speak from the heart
  - Maintain confidentiality
  - Right to pass

### 2. Check-In Round (10-15 minutes)
**Prompt Options:**
- "Share your name and something good that happened this week"
- "What's one word that describes how you're feeling today?"
- "Share a recent moment of gratitude"

### 3. Main Activity (20-30 minutes)
**Topic Options:**
- **Appreciation Round**: Share appreciation for someone in the circle
- **Story Sharing**: "Tell us about a time when you felt truly supported"
- **Values Exploration**: "What values are most important to you?"
- **Goal Setting**: "What's one thing you're working toward?"

### 4. Reflection (5-10 minutes)
**Closing Prompts:**
- "What's one thing you're taking away from today's circle?"
- "How are you feeling now compared to when we started?"

### 5. Closing (5 minutes)
- Thank participants
- Remind of next circle date
- Optional: closing ritual (poem, quote, moment of silence)

## Facilitator Notes
- Pace the circle to allow everyone time to speak
- Model vulnerability and authenticity
- Be comfortable with silence
- Gently redirect if someone dominates
- Watch for non-verbal cues of discomfort

## Materials Needed
- Talking piece
- Circle guidelines poster
- Timer
- Optional: tissues, water

## Follow-Up
- Document attendance (for participation tracking)
- Note any themes or concerns that emerged
- Plan next circle based on group needs
